Supply & Demand Analyzer

Compare how asking rents in the market are spread across price bands against the price points of our demand and our supply, broken down by bedroom type.

Step 1 Market Supply — upload data

A CSV or Excel file with Asking Rent and Bedrooms columns, one row per listing.

Step 2 Our Demand — enter data

For each bedroom type, enter the price points we have demand for and how many units at each. Add a row per price point — percentages are worked out within each bedroom type, the same way as market supply. Leave a bedroom type blank to leave it out of the charts.

Step 3 Our Supply — enter data

For each bedroom type, enter the price points we are supplying and how many units at each. Add a row per price point. Leave a bedroom type blank to leave it out of the charts.

Step 4 Price bands

Used for market supply, our demand and our supply. A band covers its lower value up to — but not including — its upper value, so £1,500 falls in £1,500–£1,800. Anything outside every band is counted as "Other".
