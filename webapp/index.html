Supply & Demand Analyzer

Compare how asking rents are spread across price bands (supply) against the price points you have demand for, broken down by bedroom type.

Step 1 Supply — upload data

A CSV or Excel file with Asking Rent and Bedrooms columns, one row per listing.

Step 2 Demand — enter data

For each bedroom type, enter the price points you have demand for and how many units at each. Add a row per price point — demand percentages are worked out within each bedroom type, the same way as supply. Leave a bedroom type blank to chart supply only.

Price bands

Used for both supply and demand. A band covers its lower value up to — but not including — its upper value, so £1,500 falls in £1,500–£1,800. Anything outside every band is counted as "Other".
